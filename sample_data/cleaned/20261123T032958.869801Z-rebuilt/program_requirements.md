# Software Engineering Systems, MS (Boston)

2026-2027 Academic Catalog

[Source](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/graduate/engineering/multidisciplinary/software-engineering-systems-ms/)

Complete all courses and requirements listed below unless otherwise indicated.

## Core Requirements

- The College of Engineering requires that students must earn a grade of C or higher in all required core courses to fulfill degree requirements. Prerequisite conditions of individual core courses may require a higher minimum grade.

- CSYE 6200 | Concepts of Object-Oriented Design | 4

- CSYE 7230 | Software Engineering | 4

- INFO 6205 | Program Structure and Algorithms | 4

## Options

Complete one of the following options:

### Coursework Option

- Complete 8 semester hours from the restricted electives course list below. | 8

- Complete 12 semester hours from the other electives course list below. | 12

### Project Option

- CSYE 7945 | Master's Project | 4

- Complete 4 semester hours from the restricted electives course list below. | 4

- Complete 12 semester hours from the other electives course list below. | 12

### Thesis Option

- CSYE 7945 | Master's Project | 4

- CSYE 7990 | Thesis | 4

- Complete 12 semester hours from the other electives course list below. | 12

- In addition to completing the thesis course, students must successfully complete the thesis submission process, including securing committee and Graduate School of Engineering signatures and submission of an electronic copy of their MS thesis to ProQuest.

#### Restricted Electives

- Complete restricted elective requirements from the following subject code:

- CSYE

#### Other Electives

- Complete the remaining minimum program required semester hours from the following subject codes:

- CSYE

- DAMG

- INFO (INFO 6250 excluded)

- TELE

## Optional Co-op Experience

- Complete the following (students must complete ENCP 6000 to qualify for co-op experience):

- ENCP 6000 | Career Management for Engineers | 1

- ENCP 6964 | Co-op Work Experience | 0

- or ENCP 6954 | Co-op Work Experience - Half-Time

- or ENCP 6955 | Co-op Work Experience Abroad - Half-Time

- or ENCP 6965 | Co-op Work Experience Abroad

## Program Credit/GPA Requirements

32 total semester hours required (33 with optional co-op) Minimum 3.000 GPA required
